## 1. Act contract

**Title:** Nobody Opens a Harbour Alone.

**Practical goal:** Prepare a coordinated, witnessed alternative to exclusive central dispatch before the next major tide.

**Emotional movement:** Nell stops treating help as something she borrows and starts treating other people as partners entitled to understand and refuse her plan. Mara moves from admitting concealment to reopening the place she sealed. Rook shares responsibility for family machinery. Vane offers Nell genuine independence at everyone else's expense.

**Opening question:** “Who will actually be there when the centre lets go?”

**Closing fact:** Three stations have named attendants, inspected local equipment, matched current observation records and a tested communications procedure. Vane has begun the announced centralisation sequence, but exclusive dispatch is not yet executed. Nell must reach the inspection bypass in Act V.

**Scope:** nine returning harbour rooms, one new walkable room beneath Repair Quay, five new close-up compositions, seven core puzzles (one has three station substeps), no new named speaking characters. Bram participates through an explicitly tested maintenance telephone. Nell remains the sole playable character. Proposed exploratory length: 75–100 minutes, to be established by playtesting rather than treated as a production estimate.

### Required outcomes

1. Reconcile Act III's operating copy with separate access and fee records without exaggerating what any source proves.
2. Explain the public-service condition in the company's fictional charter without treating a quotation as a switch or a court verdict.
3. Publish a sourced account, including the company's response and Nell's disclosed role.
4. Obtain informed commitments from the actual station attendants and witnesses.
5. Open the old quay station through an authorised, mechanically safe route.
6. Obtain a common current observation set and prepare three **disengaged** local controls.
7. Test named readbacks and the public signal legend without moving live gates.
8. Hear and refuse Vane's substantial offer; confirm departure into the next chapter.

**Reserved for Act V:** entering the central command area, the final evidence-based word duel, live bypass isolation, actual local takeover, Vane's emergency-authority broadcast, adjudication of the charter, publication of the final usable chart and Nell's survey-boat epilogue. Act IV does not sink boats, stop tides, arrest Vane or award Nell a ship.

## 2. Detailed story sequence and dependency graph

### Beat 1 — A plan is not a crew

At Repair Quay, Nell reads the notice announcing the next-major-tide dispatch watch. It now names **Watch D / current observation revision 1**. This is a proposed new notice, not an alteration of R19. Nell opens her retained procedure: the station columns are blank. A short opening exchange with Mara, who has come down from the tavern to meet the returning expedition, makes the goal concrete. After dismissal Mara walks back to the tavern; her temporary quay presence is removed, not duplicated.

Two investigation branches become available immediately. Nell can build the public case through Pip, Fine Print and Vane's public documents, or begin the practical work through Rook and Mara. Neither branch depends on choosing the right conversational tone.

### Beat 2 — Prove a policy, not a conspiracy

Pip has retained dispatch slips from the **trial watch** and printed the tariff supplement. Fine Print identifies the public-service clause and insists on reading its qualifications. The records show that a non-contracted vessel was held for an access condition while an equivalent company movement was cleared. The fee schedule conditions future ordinary dispatch on a subscription; it is not evidence that all maintenance charges are forbidden. R17 establishes recorded trial routing, R18 establishes Nell's calibration contribution, and R19 remains pending. Nell discloses that she accepted a commission for an accurate operational copy.

Pip prepares a proof with references and a bounded claim. Vane is asked for comment and gives an intelligible safety argument. Publication becomes evidence the harbour can discuss, not a magic reputational attack that opens locked doors.

### Beat 3 — Ask people, reopen a place

Rook supplies an annotated service supplement, a captive-link maintenance tool and explicit limits: inspect and test the local side, do not engage it. Mara agrees to attend the quay station and produces her maintenance key. She accompanies Nell to the old dry access door and admits why she sealed it after the relief-boat failure. Nell checks that the chamber is dry and that the door is on the personnel side of the works, then asks Mara to unlock it. The rusty grate is not a floodgate and opening it changes no water route.

Inside, the old local station is ordinary and neglected: a glazed inspection tube, isolated test loop, signal termination box and a chair with Mara's name on its old watch card. The scene gets time to breathe before another joke.

### Beat 4 — The water has not read our demonstration

Nell uses a repaired, isolated messenger branch to collect a **latched observation snapshot** from all three stations. The readings belong to one announced watch and revision; they are not three observations taken on unrelated journeys. Each local gauge must still be inspected in person. A tide schedule shows different levels from Act III's Watch C model. A routing card translates those actual readings into staged local settings.

Mara attends Repair Quay, Rook attends Outer Landing, and Quibble attends Service Inlet after instruction and a competence check. Fine Print records the common roster and communications readbacks at the quay. Orin is deliberately not assigned to a stationary gate: he must captain the voyage. No one is asked to operate two stations or be in two rooms at once.

### Beat 5 — Make readiness audible

The attendants consent, repeat their station-specific instructions and perform disconnected tests. Nell tests the maintenance telephone to Bram, then compares the current roster with every station's returned watch, revision and status. The harbour signal board explains the difference between a test, a central emergency assertion and an acknowledged local handover. A public test proves the signal path works; nobody hears a test bell and changes a gate.

### Beat 6 — The good offer

Once the evidence proof is verified and local preparations are coherent, Vane invites Nell to her office. The offer is real: a fitted survey vessel, salary and control of the survey office, with exclusive operational publication rights retained by Fair Passage. Nell can inspect every term, challenge it or leave to think. The fixed story proceeds only on an explicit refusal, not because the player accidentally selects a polite line.

Vane argues that a committee cannot react as quickly as one director. Nell answers that the stations have named responsibility and shared information, not an absence of responsibility. Vane does not confess to unrelated historical harm. She acknowledges the comparison documents and disputes their policy conclusion.

### Beat 7 — Announcement, not execution

Back at Repair Quay, Nell reviews the preparations with Fine Print. The player can still explore and correct them. On **Commit the harbour plan**, all readiness predicates are rechecked. A short illustrated transition shows Vane leave the office, board her company launch and announce the centralisation sequence from Bellweather. This scripted travel explicitly separates her movements from Nell's.

The signal is **CENTRALISATION ANNOUNCED — EXECUTION PENDING**, not the emergency signal reserved for Act V. Attendants hold their stations and do not engage local controls. Orin offers passage from Blind Inlet. The chapter ends before Nell embarks; no real-time timer starts. If Act V is not implemented, the boundary says so and preserves exploration and saving.

**Fingerprint:** watch + observation revision + each station's named attendant, level and detent. A rehearsal is valid only if its stored fingerprint equals the current state. A person's consent survives equipment edits; their technical acknowledgement does not.

Preserve invalid raw data to a recovery key with explicit user action, following earlier acts' recovery pattern.

### Save/reload boundaries

- Mid-dialogue: replay current mandatory block or restore its acknowledgement state without repeating relocations.
- Mid-repair: retain fitted link and isolation/test state; no duplicate part grant.
- Partial stations: retain correct previous work, show remaining tasks.
- After selector edit: restore stale acknowledgement as stale, not valid.
- Mid-offer: undecided remains undecided; “heard offer” is not “declined.”
- During final overlay: one atomic commit records Vane departure/announcement/completion; interrupted playback can resume without executing the event twice.
- Completed exploration: maintain all safety invariants. Preparation controls become read-only review panels after commitment, so the completed handoff cannot be silently invalidated by casual selector changes. Optional dialogue and eggs remain available where characters still are.
