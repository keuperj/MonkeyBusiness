## 1. Act contract

**Title:** The People Who Kept Quiet.

**Practical goal:** Discover how to stop exclusive central control without leaving the harbour's gates uncontrolled.

**Emotional movement:** Nell moves from admitting her mistake to accepting help without surrendering her judgement. Mara moves from protecting people through concealment to sharing knowledge and responsibility.

**Opening question:** “What did we help them finish?”

**Closing discovery:** The Tide Ledger records its commands. Its documented emergency procedure permits coordinated local control using fresh, matching measurements and explicit station acknowledgements. A successful dry demonstration proves the principle, not that the harbour is already prepared.

**Scope:** Four new walkable shoal backgrounds, nine existing harbour backgrounds available for revisits, a voyage overlay rather than a separate walkable ship deck, and three new close-ups: departure comparison, recorder service tray, and disconnected training board. One new speaking character, Bram. All eight existing speakers have defined Act III roles and optional lines below. Target 60–90 minutes of exploratory play, subject to playtesting—not a production estimate.

### Required outcomes

1. Nell tells Mara precisely what she gave Vane; Mara acknowledges her own earlier concealment.
2. Learn about the stranded relief boat without blaming Vane for an incident not yet connected to her.
3. Arrange a named, acknowledged harbour watch rather than assigning work to absent allies.
4. Plot a service approach with current observations and the retained survey.
5. Reach an ordinary public-works installation behind the shoal's grand reputation.
6. Meet Bram, follow the inspection route and identify what the central drum does.
7. Copy the operating record without altering or disabling the live gate controls.
8. Distinguish a replaceable recorder component from the live coordinating drum.
9. Demonstrate coordinated local control on a disconnected model; carry home the procedure and evidence.

**Do not resolve yet:** station staffing, fresh measurements for the actual takeover, fee-record comparison, a completed public accusation, the legal safe-passage argument, Vane's counteroffer, access to the live central controls, or the final override. Those remain Acts IV–V. No dialogue choice cancels a contract, changes a gate remotely or proves a motive by itself.
