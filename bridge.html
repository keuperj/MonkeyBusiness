Development notes & controls

Choose a comeback, then continue to the next exchange. Sound enables voiced characters and harbour ambience. Restart clears only this duel; returning to the tavern preserves your room progress for this tab. The duel is turn-based and has no timer. Animations use generated character art, independently pivoting swords, fencing lunges, parries, recoil and parrot swoops.
