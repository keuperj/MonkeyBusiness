## Entry and continuity

Complete Act II and choose **Continue to Act III**, or use **Continue Act III** on the start menu. Direct entry through `/act3.html` starts the canonical chapter state if no Act III autosave exists. No earlier save is overwritten.

Visit Outer Landing and inspect the departure board, warehouse corner/lighthouse notch, and tethered float. In the departure comparison choose:

Record the plan. At Blind Inlet review the checklist and sail with Orin. Confirm the voyage and continue the travel overlay.

## Two independent investigation branches

In the gallery inspect the system diagram. Optional: talk with Bram and read the historical incident binder. The old incident does not identify Vane as its operator.

**Recorder:** read the plain part label. Open the service close-up, choose SERVICE and the two-tab / blue-triangle collar. Fit it, continue servicing, choose RUN, and print. The used part stays on the bench. Read the operating copy and select only the first three supported claims; the pending request has not executed.

**Overlook:** read the inspection bypass diagram. The model is disconnected from live gates. Set Watch C at every station, Low at Outer Landing and Middle at the other two stations. Set LOCAL READY everywhere, request acknowledgements, isolate MODEL CENTRAL, then run. Changing any selector cancels every acknowledgement and the isolation.

## Synthesis and ending

Choose **Bring the evidence together**. Select the first three actions: retain live control until cover exists, gather current readings/acknowledgements, and compare the record against access notices/fees. Do not select sabotage, training values for real gates, or unsupported accusations.

At Service Landing choose **Return with the plan**. Cancelling keeps all evidence. Confirming returns the boat to Blind Inlet and walks Nell to Repair Quay. Continue both overlays. The completion boundary honestly states that Act IV is not yet available; further exploration and saving remain possible.
