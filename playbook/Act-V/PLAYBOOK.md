# Act V playbook — The Right of Way

## Verified through completion and reload

Captured on 7 November 2026 in one fresh browser session using normal controls. No debug presets, injected saves or progression edits were used. Direct Act V entry supplies the canonical completed-Act-IV inventory, including lint; this capture does not independently verify an imported Act IV save. Sound was muted using the game control and reduced motion enabled for reproducible captures. These screenshots verify written dialogue and gameplay, not audio quality or animation timing.

**Full spoilers**, including Exhibit Zero, safe reverse recovery and the cinematic/credits ending. 65 screenshots.

## Controls

Use **Map** for travel on the current shore and select the circled destination. Map captures temporarily hide destination buttons, then restore them. Choose **Continue** on travel overlays. Crossing the sea requires **Check voyage with Orin**, the three current sources and explicit confirmation.

Select **Look at**, expand **Room objects · keyboard controls**, and select the named target. Selecting an inventory item switches to Use; select Look again for inspections. **Back to the room** restores the room actions. Screenshots temporarily expand the response panel so long evidence remains readable.

## Recovery and optional content

Wrong choices do not consume evidence. Station edits invalidate the affected test/readback and combined witness token. Recovery reverses active local pilots before restoring central command and clears technical acknowledgements; renew all three and the witness record afterward. The illustrated route deliberately demonstrates recovery from INLET_LOCAL_HOLD. This detour is optional.

An emergency declaration is not independent hazard proof. Inspect all four current reports, retain warnings and assistance, and only then continue the checked sequence. Never reconnect central over an engaged local pilot. Save and autosave are separate.

**Exhibit Zero** is the only new state-tracked Act V Easter egg; it requires retained lint and never consumes it. It has a replay and boat-inventory callback. The credits are a separate presentation joke, not a puzzle or discovery flag. See [the Easter-egg guide](../EasterEggs.md).
