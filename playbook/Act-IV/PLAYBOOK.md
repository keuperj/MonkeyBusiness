# Act IV playbook — Nobody Opens a Harbour Alone

## Verified through completion and reload

Captured on 6 November 2026 in one fresh browser session using normal controls. No debug presets, injected saves or progression edits were used. Direct Act IV entry supplies the canonical completed-Act-III inventory, including lint; this capture does not independently verify an imported Act III save. Sound was muted using the game control, and reduced motion enabled for reproducible captures. These screenshots verify written dialogue and gameplay, not audio quality.

**Full spoilers**, including all three optional Easter eggs. 66 screenshots.

## Controls

Use **Map** for voluntary travel and select the circled destination. Travel screenshots show only the illustrated map; destination controls are hidden for capture and immediately restored. Choose **Continue** on route overlays. Mara’s and Rook’s meeting actions use their explicit travel controls.

Select **Look at**, expand **Room objects · keyboard controls**, and select the named target. **Back to the room** restores actions after dialogue. Selecting an inventory item switches to Use; select **Look at** again before inspections. Paper items have distinct symbol badges and retain their names/tooltips.

## Station solution at a glance

| Station | Attendant | Watch / revision | Level | Detent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Outer Landing | Rook | D / 1 | High | B |
| Repair Quay dry chamber | Mara | D / 1 | Low | A |
| Service Inlet at Blind Inlet | Quibble | D / 1 | Low | C |

Inspect each local gauge, stage settings, run the disconnected test with Rook’s handle, and obtain a named readback. Editing any setting invalidates that station’s test/readback and the global rehearsal: retest, request its readback, then repeat the public rehearsal. Nothing in Act IV authorises a live gate change.

## Recovery and optional content

Wrong selections do not consume evidence. If the proof is unavailable, finish evidence and charter comparisons and obtain Vane’s response. If the chamber is locked, bring Mara to the quay and inspect the dry telltale and warning. If a readback fails, check the attendant’s recruitment, local gauge, current snapshot and passed test. The offer requires publication and a witnessed rehearsal. The final review identifies missing requirements. Manual Save/Load and autosave are separate.

The mascot, appointment-clock and maintenance-credit discoveries are optional; the clock detour requires retained lint, which is not consumed. See also [the Easter-egg guide](../EasterEggs.md).
