# Act I Playbook — Step-by-step solution to Act II

**A Job, a Tankard and a Toll**  
**Status: completed and verified by an actual browser playthrough on 6 November 2026.**

> **Full puzzle and dialogue spoilers.** This guide follows one successful route from the title screen through Act I and into Act II’s Repair Quay.

## How this guide was verified

Every numbered step below was performed in order using the player-facing UI in Chromium, with a fresh browser profile. No debug starts, injected saves, console progression changes, or test fixtures were used. Scene pickups and item combinations were performed by clicking the canvas. Conversations, answer choices, travel, and evidence selection used the normal visible controls. The run ended at Repair Quay with Nell’s Act II recap visible.

**No gameplay problem was encountered on this route.** This is a verified solution, not an exhaustive test of every optional interaction or alternative route. Sound was off during this playthrough; the guide verifies visual/text progression, not audio quality. Optional Easter eggs and extra conversations are not required for this route and are omitted.

## Reading the screenshots

- Each screenshot shows the screen **before** performing that step. The final success screenshot shows the destination reached.
- Yellow rectangles mark the controls or objects to click. Numbered circles match the legend below each image; use them in order.
- Select **Pick up** with the hand icon, **Look at** with the magnifying glass, and inventory items from **Your pockets & papers**. Selecting an inventory item prepares a Use action.
- Click the destination emblems in the upper-right header to travel. **Continue** on the map skips the journey; normal-motion travel also finishes automatically.
- Duel answer order is shuffled. Match the **wording**, not the screenshot’s answer position.
- Wait for animations and chapter downloads to finish before continuing. The prologue and venue introductions can be watched or skipped using their on-screen buttons.

## Route at a glance

1. **Tavern:** return the tankard → receive key → unlock chest → take survey → take boathook.
2. **Bridge:** win at least three of five word exchanges. This guide wins all five.
3. **Outer Landing:** read the tide board → inspect fixed landmarks → compare the survey → free the float → observe the signal/current/launch sequence.
4. **Conclusion:** record the three supported facts → choose the signal-hardware lead → continue to Act II.

## The Crooked Compass: survey and equipment

### 04. Ask Mara about work

Click Talk to Mara in the right-hand action panel.

![Step 04: Ask Mara about work. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/04.png)

### 05. Learn how to earn the chest key

Ask what is in the sea chest. Mara will trade its key for her brass tankard.

![Step 05: Learn how to earn the chest key. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/05.png)

### 06. Pick up the brass tankard

Choose Pick up (the hand icon), then click the brass tankard on the window table. It appears in your pockets.

![Step 06: Pick up the brass tankard. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/06.png)

### 07. Return the tankard to Mara

Select the brass tankard in your pockets, then click Mara. She gives you the brass key.

![Step 07: Return the tankard to Mara. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/07.png)

### 08. Unlock the sea chest

Select the brass key, then click the chest at the left side of the room. Opening the chest does not automatically take the survey.

![Step 08: Unlock the sea chest. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/08.png)

### 09. Take the old survey

Choose Pick up, then click the rolled survey inside the open chest. Check that Old survey appears in your pockets.

![Step 09: Take the old survey. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/09.png)

### 10. Take the boathook

Keep Pick up selected and click the long boathook mounted above the fireplace. Carry it with you for the landing puzzle.

![Step 10: Take the boathook. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/10.png)

## Quibble’s Crossing: win passage

### 11. Travel to Quibble’s Crossing

Click the bridge destination emblem at the upper right. On the travel map, Continue skips the journey.

![Step 11: Travel to Quibble’s Crossing. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/11.png)

### 12. Challenge the bridge keepers

Click Challenge the bridge keepers. Win at least three of five word exchanges. Answers are shuffled, so select by wording, not position.

![Step 12: Challenge the bridge keepers. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/12.png)

### 13. Win exchange 1: reputation

Choose: “Then I’ll fight your reputation while you catch up.” Wait for the fencing animation, then click Next exchange.

![Step 13: Win exchange 1: reputation. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/13.png)

### 14. Win exchange 2: the parrot’s bill

Choose: “Put it on your bill. You’ve already got one.” Then click Next exchange.

![Step 14: Win exchange 2: the parrot’s bill. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/14.png)

### 15. Win exchange 3: knots

Choose: “Wonderful. I’ve been looking for a bow tie with ambition.” This earns the third point; continue through the remaining exchanges.

![Step 15: Win exchange 3: knots. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/15.png)

### 16. Win exchange 4: royalties

Choose: “Then these unpaid taxes belong to you too.” Then click Next exchange.

![Step 16: Win exchange 4: royalties. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/16.png)

### 17. Win exchange 5: the last word

Choose: “Say welcome. You get the last word; I get across.” Click the enabled next/result button after the animation to finish the duel.

![Step 17: Win exchange 5: the last word. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/17.png)

## Outer Landing: establish the evidence

### 18. Cross to the Outer Landing

Click Continue to the landing, then Continue on the travel map. You should still have the old survey and boathook.

![Step 18: Cross to the Outer Landing. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/18.png)

### 19. Read the tide board

Choose Look at (magnifying glass), then click the tide board on the left. Record that the expected tide is ebb, flowing left.

![Step 19: Read the tide board. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/19.png)

### 20. Inspect the fixed landmarks

With Look at selected, click the distant warehouse and lighthouse area. These fixed features provide reliable survey references.

![Step 20: Inspect the fixed landmarks. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/20.png)

### 21. Compare the survey at the sighting cleat

Select Old survey in your pockets, then click the sighting cleat at the centre of the dock.

![Step 21: Compare the survey at the sighting cleat. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/21.png)

### 22. Choose the two fixed marks

Click Warehouse corner and Lighthouse notch. Do not choose Company buoy: a floating object is not a fixed reference.

![Step 22: Choose the two fixed marks. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/22.png)

### 23. Free the current indicator

Select Boathook in your pockets, then click the tethered float on the right. Wait for Nell to lift its line off the bolt.

![Step 23: Free the current indicator. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/23.png)

### 24. Begin the channel observation

Click Watch the channel. Read the first observation before moving on.

![Step 24: Begin the channel observation. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/24.png)

### 25. Observe the normal ebb

The float initially settles left, matching the tide board. Click Keep watching.

![Step 25: Observe the normal ebb. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/25.png)

### 26. Observe the signal

Note the three amber flashes and three bell strokes. Click Keep watching to see what follows the signal.

![Step 26: Observe the signal. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/26.png)

### 27. Observe the changed current

The float and leaves move right even though the board still says ebb. Click Keep watching.

![Step 27: Observe the changed current. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/27.png)

### 28. Record the launch using the new route

The company launch immediately follows the changed route. Click Record what happened to preserve the full observation.

![Step 28: Record the launch using the new route. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/28.png)

### 29. Review the evidence

Click Review the evidence. Separate what Nell directly observed from unsupported accusations.

![Step 29: Review the evidence. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/29.png)

### 30. Select only the three supported statements

Check the first three statements: the tide board still indicated ebb; the signal immediately preceded the change; and a company launch used the changed route without hesitation. Leave the claims about controlling the entire sea and stealing Orin’s ship unchecked. Click Record these observations.

![Step 30: Select only the three supported statements. Numbered yellow highlights identify the relevant controls and objects.](screenshots/30.png)
